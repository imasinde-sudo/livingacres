Our Farm

Good Food Begins at the Farm.

Living Acres by ESS is a Kenyan poultry business focused on raising quality birds and producing fresh eggs for households and commercial customers. Our approach brings together attentive farming, responsible management and a commitment to making quality poultry products accessible.

From day-old chicks in the brooder, to growers on clean deep litter, to birds foraging in the yard — every stage is raised by hand.
